### Joomla extensions I maintain

Free and open source, kept up to date for Joomla 5 and 6:

- [**Google Tag Manager**](https://github.com/hans2103/plg_system_googletagmanager): GTM with Consent Mode v2, a native cookie consent banner and Google tag gateway support
- [**ImageKit**](https://github.com/hans2103/plg_system_imagekit): images through the ImageKit.io CDN with automatic `srcset` and WebP/AVIF
- [**YouTube**](https://github.com/hans2103/plg_content_youtube): privacy-friendly YouTube embeds that load only after a click
- [**Error 404**](https://github.com/hans2103/plg_system_error404): custom, multilingual 404 pages that can't be deleted by accident
- [**Sanitize filename**](https://github.com/hans2103/plg_media-action_sanitizefilename): clean, lowercase filenames on upload
- [**Max size**](https://github.com/hans2103/plg_media-action_maxsize): resize images that are too large on upload

Issues and pull requests are welcome.
